01 · Brand conceptClinicle
The big idea

Three pillars.
One platform.

Clinicle exists to bring three audiences out of their silos and into one community: the people receiving care, the people delivering it, and the people paying for it. The identity makes that promise visible.

Patients

People, families and members. The smaller dot — closest to us, where the journey starts.

Providers

Clinicians, clinics, hospitals. The largest dot at the apex — anchoring the network.

Payers

Insurers, employers, plans. The mint accent — the connector that completes the orbit.

Why Orbit?

The mark resolves all three audiences as a single shape — three dots arranged in a gentle upward arc, like planets sharing one orbit. None dominates. None drifts. Read together they form a single rising movement: three parties getting better, together. The mint dot — Payers — is placed last on the right, as the connector that completes the system.

Why all-light?

Healthcare brands lean dark and clinical by default. Clinicle deliberately drops black ink — including in the wordmark itself — and stays in sky blue and mint teal. The result feels human, hopeful, and modern; trustworthy without feeling cold.

The mark, the wordmark, the lockups

Anatomy.

Clinicle
connecting health. empowering lives

The mark

Three dots in an upward arc — Patients (small sky blue), Providers (large deep sky) and Payers (mint teal accent). Always rendered in pure circles; never connected by lines or strokes.

The wordmark

"Clinicle" set in Nunito 800. The stem "Clinic" in Deep Sky #2E9FDC; the suffix "le" in Mint Teal #5EDEC8 — the same accent that lives in the mark, completing the system.

The tagline

"connecting health. empowering lives" set in Inter Tight 500, all caps, tracked +180. Always sits below the entire mark + wordmark line, never beside or above.

04 · Clear space & sizingClinicle

Give it room.

The minimum clear space around the logo equals the diameter of the largest dot in the mark (the deep-sky Provider dot, x). Maintain x on every side. Don't let any other element — text, photo edge, container border — cross into this protected zone.

05 · ColorClinicle

Sky & Mint.

The full Clinicle palette is six tones — built from sky blues that anchor and mint that connects. No black, no navy, no warm grays. Use Deep Sky as the primary color; Mint Teal as the accent; Sky Mist for whitespace; Sky Light and Mint Soft as supporting tints.

Color usage rules

  • Deep Sky is the brand's voice. Use it for primary text, the wordmark body, and key UI accents.
  • Mint Teal is reserved as the accent — the connector. Use it sparingly: the "le" suffix in the wordmark, a single button, a subtle highlight. Never as a body color.
  • Sky Mist is the default secondary surface. Avoid pure gray backgrounds — let sky tones breathe.
  • Never combine the wordmark with black, navy, charcoal or warm earth tones.

Nunito & Inter Tight.

Two open-source families. Nunito carries the brand: rounded, warm, optimistic. Inter Tight handles UI text and tracked-out details. Both are free, screen-tuned, and load fast.

Type rules

  • Headlines are always Nunito 700–800. Never use weight 300/400 for display.
  • Body copy is Inter Tight 400. Line-height 1.55. Color: #4d7e9b (Deep Sky tinted for paragraphs).
  • Wordmark spacing is fixed at letter-spacing −1.5%. Don't re-set it.
  • Tagline tracking is +180 (0.18em). Always uppercase.

07 · Voice & toneClinicle

How Clinicle speaks.

Warm, plainspoken, optimistic. Clinicle is a healthcare brand that doesn't sound like one. We trade jargon for human language; we trade clinical for clear.

We are

  • Warm — never cold or institutional
  • Plain — never jargon-filled
  • Confident — never overpromising
  • Optimistic — never naive
  • Inclusive — never one-sided to a single audience

We are not

  • Salesy
  • Bureaucratic
  • Pessimistic about healthcare
  • Technical for technical's sake
  • Cute

Examples

Yes

"One platform. Three connected pillars."

Clear, confident, and grounded in the brand idea.

No

"Empowering stakeholders to leverage synergies in the healthcare value chain."

Jargon. Says nothing. Sounds like every other healthcare deck.

Sample headlines

  • Where patients, providers and payers connect.
  • Health, finally on the same page.
  • One sign-in for everyone in the room.
  • Care that flows — across systems, across roles.
